## Purpose

This document is the source of truth for presenting Josué Amaral's small,
fixed-scope software services on Fiverr. It separates the complete service
inventory from the smaller set of Gigs that can be published at launch.

The offers must remain truthful, technically bounded and compatible with the
public professional portfolio. They must not promise a result outside the
freelancer's control, request off-platform payment, expose contact details, or
imply access to systems without the client's authorization.

## Current Fiverr constraints

The following constraints were verified in Fiverr's official Help Center on
2026-10-07:

- Gig creation is available only in a desktop browser.
- A new freelancer can maintain at most 4 Gigs; Levels 1 and 2 can maintain 10,
  and Top Rated freelancers can maintain 30.
- A Gig needs a title, category, scope and pricing, English description,
  requirements and at least one gallery image.
- Up to three packages may be offered. Each package needs a price in USD,
  delivery time and at least one revision.
- A description may contain up to 1,200 characters and a Gig may use up to five
  search tags and ten FAQs.
- The starting price is normally USD 5, but some categories impose a higher
  minimum.
- Fiverr credits the freelancer with 80% of a completed order, including
  extras and tips. The normal revenue-clearance period is 14 days after order
  completion.
- Contact and payment must stay on Fiverr. A portfolio link may demonstrate
  experience, but it cannot be used to move business off-platform.
- Automation that violates another platform's terms, unauthorized access,
  collection of private or personal data, fake engagement and evasion of
  access controls are prohibited.

## Commercial safeguards

- Do not accept an order whose scope cannot be evaluated from the buyer's
  requirements; propose discovery or a custom offer instead.
- Never put secrets, personal data or production credentials in a public Gig,
  screenshot, repository or delivery report.
- Keep business communication and payment on Fiverr.
- Require authorization before accessing repositories, infrastructure or
  third-party systems.
- Prefer redacted fixtures, sandbox credentials, staging and reversible changes.
- State exclusions and third-party costs before the order begins.
- Do not promise rankings, revenue, model accuracy, recovery of suspended
  accounts, successful deployment on an unsupported environment, or any result
  outside the agreed technical deliverable.
- Price urgent work only after availability and reproducibility are confirmed.
- Record what was tested; do not equate a local test with production success.
